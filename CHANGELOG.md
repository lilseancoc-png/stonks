## 2026-07-04

### Changed
- **Top Picks moves to its own role-gated page; the old tab becomes "Market analysis".** The picks roster (grouped cards, sort, summary chips, CSV export, live board, how-it-works) now lives on a separate **Top picks** tab that is role-hidden exactly like Track Record: a new `tp` session claim minted from `DISCORD_TOPPICKS_ROLE_ID(S)` (dormant when unset — every member keeps access) hides the nav button/landing card/palette entry and `api/data` 401s `picks.json` + `picks-open.json` without the role. Everything else on the old tab — the live market tape, the risk-on/risk-off barometer, the regime-history calendar, the regime-drift banner, the grade-any-ticker search, and the "check a position you hold" tool — stays on the renamed **Market analysis** tab (plain premium). The bake + `regen-picks` now also write `data/market-analysis.json` (premium, NOT role-restricted — the same `macroRegime` object that rides `picks.json`'s `rosterMeta`) so the Market analysis tab works for members without the picks role; `lib/premium-keys.mjs` grows a per-key role-claim map (`roleClaimForKey`: `tr` vs `tp`). `scripts/render/{html,app-js,docs}.mjs`, `scripts/build.mjs`, `scripts/regen-picks.mjs`, `lib/premium-keys.mjs`, `api/auth/[action].js`, `api/data/[...path].js`, `welcome.html`, `CLAUDE.md`.

### Perf
- **Cross-build cache for the per-ticker AI news+fundamentals judgment — the build's biggest Gemini cost center.** `attachTickerJudgments` (one Flash-Lite call per ticker per bake, each carrying up to 10 headlines with article bodies, 8 bakes/day) now caches each ticker's result in `data/ticker-judgment-cache.json`, keyed on the ET date + the raw headline set + the slow-moving fundamentals facts (last quarter, next earnings date, consensus) + the models; price-derived prompt drift is excluded from the key, and a reused take is re-read once spot drifts >2% (`AI_TICKER_CACHE_DRIFT`) from the price it was generated at. Intraday, unchanged-news names now reuse the prior build's judgment on bakes 2–8 — skipping the Gemini call AND the article body-fetch wave. The same cache entry also stores the split Major-Contract/Guidance extraction (`attachAiContractGuidance`), so that second per-ticker call is skipped on unchanged headlines too while `AI_SIGNALS_COMBINED` stays off. Read-before-wipe / write-after-wipe like the chart-pattern cache; keyless builds carry it forward; failures and the zero-headline fallback are never cached. `AI_TICKER_CACHE=0` disables. `scripts/build.mjs`, `lib/premium-keys.mjs` (new key gated as internal bookkeeping), `CLAUDE.md`.

## 2026-07-01

### Added
- **The hourly market brief gains a "Tickers to watch" section — a deterministic single-name event/news screen over the whole universe, minted fresh with every brief.** Deliberately NOT sourced from the Top Picks roster (roster overlap is badged "also a top pick" instead): each curated name scores from its per-ticker AI news take (bullish/bearish only — the keyless/no-news fallback is "uncertain" and never fires), dated near-term catalysts from the news read (FDA/court/launch/merger…), fresh capital-raise/buyback headlines, earnings timing (today's AM/PM session or inside ~2 days), unusual options flow, heavy relative volume (≥2x 20D), big 1-day moves, and 52-week-extreme touches. A name needs at least one event-ish reason (news/catalyst/raise/earnings/flow) or a 2-signal tape pile-up, so the block doesn't just mirror the biggest-movers strip; broad index ETFs are excluded; ships ≤6 names with reason chips + the news/catalyst clause in the tooltip, each clickable through to the Grade tab. The list also rides the AI prompt so the brief's prose can weave the interesting names into the narrative. `scripts/build.mjs` (`buildBriefWatchlist` exported, `gatherBriefSignals`/`briefRenderFields`/`briefUserMessage`, `BRIEF_WATCH_MAX`/`BRIEF_WATCH_EXCLUDE`), `scripts/render/app-js.mjs` + `app.js` (`renderBriefCard` watchlist block), `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Track Record gains a "Summary" sub-tab — a rules-based engine report that explains how the picks engine is performing, what needs work, and why each pick won or lost.** Now the tab's default landing view. Everything is deterministic (computed from the resolved record only — no AI, open marks excluded): a **health verdict** banner (Healthy / Mixed / Struggling / Early) with a plain-English narrative (win rate, R-expectancy, profit factor, open-book pulse); a **loss anatomy** block that classifies every loss as a direction miss vs. theta bleed vs. drift+decay off the persisted side-adjusted stock move vs. option P&L (the same taxonomy as `scripts/diagnose-pick-losses.mjs`, live on the tab), with a stacked attribution bar, exit-reason chips, the flat-but-lost count, and the direction-vs-vehicle verdict that decides where the fix belongs; a **winner anatomy** read (direction wins vs. premium-decay wins + how much of the peak mark winners kept); **what is working / what needs work** — the best and worst segments scanned across every breakdown dimension (side, conviction, thesis, structure, DTE, PoP, regime, sector; ≥4-trade buckets only, ranked by return-on-risk); a **what to fix next** list of fired diagnostics (below-breakeven win-rate÷payoff math, giveback losers that peaked +15% before resolving red, time-stop-dominated losses, stop-riding losses, conviction tiers not scaling, one-sided call/put books); and a **why each pick won or lost** feed — a one-line deterministic attribution per resolved pick, newest first. Breakdowns also gains a **Structure** dimension (naked long / debit / credit vertical) in the tables + cross-tab. `scripts/render/app-js.mjs` (`accTradeWhy`/`buildEngineReport`/`renderSummaryView`, `ACC_DIMS.structure`), `scripts/render/html.mjs` (`trackRecordSection`), `scripts/render/styles-css.mjs`.
- **Index Calendar adds an SMH toggle.** The semis ETF joins the month-grid between IWM and DIA; SMH is already a curated chain ticker, so its daily closes ride the bake's own chains and ~1yr of history backfills on the next build. `scripts/build.mjs` (`INDEX_CALENDAR_SYMBOLS`), `scripts/render/app-js.mjs` (`IDX_CAL_LABELS`/`IDX_CAL_ORDER`), `scripts/render/docs.mjs`.

### Changed
- **Top Picks exits rebalanced — scale-out + trail replaces the hard +20% win cap, the stock stop is enforced, and the credit-spread stop tightens to −50%.** The track record showed avg loss running ~4× avg win: the flat +20% take-profit hard-capped every winner at the first mark ≥ +20% while overnight/earnings gaps blew losses far past the −30% stop (gates only execute at build-time marks), a payoff needing a >60% win rate against a ~33% record. Now (1) at +20% the position **banks half at the marked price and trails the runner** — breakeven floor, ratcheting to peak−25pts (`trail-stop` status; closed records blend the two halves; `PICKS_OPT_SCALE_OUT=0` restores the flat TP, `PICKS_OPT_TRAIL_GIVEBACK_PCT` tunes the ratchet) — so an armed winner can't round-trip to a net loss but the right tail is no longer amputated; (2) the exit ladder's ~2.5×ATR/structural **stock stop is now enforced in the record** (`hit-stop-under`; it was display-only — `resolvePickOutcome` never read the stored `cut` level, so a name whose stock thesis had broken stayed open until the modeled premium printed −30% at a mark; `PICKS_UNDERLYING_STOP=0` reverts); (3) the credit-vertical stop default drops **−100% → −50% of the credit** (buy-back ≈1.5×), symmetric with the +50% take-profit (the old 2:1 inverted payoff let one stopped spread erase two winners). Exit-plan card prose + Track Record status labels updated to match. `scripts/build.mjs` (`resolvePickOutcome`, `updatePicksAccuracyFile`, `buildExitPlan`/`buildCreditExitPlan`), `scripts/render/app-js.mjs` + `app.js` (`ACC_STATUS_LABEL`, TP/cut exit counters), `scripts/picks-smoke.mjs`, `docs/top-picks.md`.
- **The market brief is now a rolling hourly digest minted by the bake, replacing the separate pre-market and post-close briefs.** `data/briefs.json` becomes `{ current }` — one brief re-minted at most once per ET hour, so every hourly build (9:30 open through the 16:00 close) ships a fresh read; a same-hour re-run carries the existing brief forward. Framing follows the clock: the open build writes a **morning** setup (overnight/futures context), mid-session builds an **intraday** where-the-tape-stands read (new kind, new system prompt, "session so far" signals), and the 16:00+ build the **afternoon** closing read. The oi-tracker workflow's `regen-brief.mjs` step (pre-market ~08:30 mint + ~19:00 backfill) is removed along with the script — the brief now rides only the bake. The Brief tab renders the single rolling card (with a legacy `{ morning, afternoon }` fallback across the cutover) and the morning-brief macro-print re-mint trigger is gone (a fresh print reaches the next hourly mint by construction). `scripts/build.mjs` (`buildMarketBriefs`/`briefKindForHour`/`briefSystemPrompt`/`gatherBriefSignals`), `scripts/render/{app-js,html,styles-css,docs}.mjs`, `.github/workflows/oi-tracker.yml`, `scripts/sync-data.mjs` (briefs/ai-usage leave the OI ownership set).
- **New "RAM prices" tab (Macro dropdown, free tier) (#527)** — DRAM/memory pricing tracked from independent website sources with % increases per window. **Wholesale spot**: the TrendForce DRAM spot table (DDR3/4/5 chips + modules, session average + daily % change), with DRAMeXchange as the fallback parse; spot sources only publish the current session, so the bake accumulates its own daily history across builds (read-before-wipe, like the other `data/` histories) to grow 7d/30d % changes over time. **US retail**: WhereIsMyRam's DDR5 kit tracker — ~15 months of embedded biweekly min/avg history per kit category joined to human-readable labels, an all-kits market aggregate (in-stock count, 7d/30d %), and a median-of-categories composite index with 7d/30d/3mo/1y % chips + a hover-crosshair trend chart. Both sides carry last-good forward (marked stale) on a fetch/parse miss. New `data/ram-prices.json` (bake-owned, FREE key); `scripts/build.mjs` (`parseTrendforceDramSpot`, `parseDramexchangeSpot`, `parseWhereIsMyRamRetail`, `buildRamPricesPayload`, `writeRamPricesFile`), `scripts/render/{html,app-js,styles-css}.mjs` (`loadRamPrices`/`renderRamPrices`, `ram-prices` tab + `ram`/`dram`/`memory`/`ddr5` aliases). `renderHistoryChart` gains an optional `formatLabel` so calendar-time series can label points by date instead of fiscal quarter.
- **AI CapEx tab gains a revenue check — combined revenue total + growth vs CapEx growth (#527).** The bake now fetches each Mag-7 name's total revenue from the same SEC XBRL companyconcept API (`Revenues` / ASC 606 contract-revenue tags, most-recent-FY-wins probe) and reduces it with the same FY+TTM reducer as capex, so the comparison sits on identical fiscal years. New hero strip: combined revenue for the latest FY vs the prior year, CapEx YoY vs revenue YoY side by side (with a "CapEx is growing N pts faster than revenue" read), and aggregate CapEx as a share of revenue (latest FY vs prior year + run-rate) — all sums restricted to names reporting both series so the ratios stay apples-to-apples. Per-company rows add revenue YoY + latest-FY revenue; the per-name intensity ratio now prefers SEC TTM revenue over the Yahoo fallback. `scripts/build.mjs` (`AI_CAPEX_REVENUE_CONCEPTS`, `buildAiCapexPayload` — per-company `rev` block + `totals.revenue`), `scripts/render/app-js.mjs` (`renderAiCapex` revenue strip), `scripts/render/html.mjs` (info note).

### Fixed
- **Track record: the weekly reset no longer censors the open book.** The reset discarded unresolved open entries outright, so a slow winner grinding toward the take-profit over 6–8 sessions vanished uncounted at the week boundary while fast gap losers resolved in-week and *were* counted — survivorship skew that inflated avg-loss vs avg-win and made the 14-day time stop unreachable (no tracked position could live past ~5 sessions). Reset builds now **force-close every open entry at its current mark** (status `reset`, win/loss by the blended P&L sign) so every enrolled pick resolves and counts. `scripts/build.mjs` (`updatePicksAccuracyFile`).
