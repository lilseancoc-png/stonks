## 2026-11-11

### Added

- Show the sportsbooks' no-vig chance beside the model's in the MCP's `sports_evaluate_entry` and `sports_get_player` (NBA, PropLine reference prices, median across books with each book listed), with a `BOOK_DISAGREES` warning at a 5-point gap and a `use_book_chances` option; both tools also show the player's ESPN injury status and teammates listed out. Read-only, through the props board's two-minute cache (`lib/sports/book-price.mjs`).
- Add **breakeven per pick** to the Pick Builder and MCP (`perPickBreakeven`: 3 picks at 6× power need 55.0% each; flex solved from the payout table) and 95% ranges on every hit rate (`wilsonRange`), so 6 of 10 reads "likely 31-83%".
- Each model forecast now says how the holdout scored at that chance (`tested`) and flags a player's first games of a season, where forecasts ran high (NBA points +1.6, NFL passing yards +16 on the holdout).
- Model 21 Sports stats instead of 4 (`sports-model-3`): NBA assists, 3-pointers, steals, blocks, turnovers and the PRA / points + rebounds / points + assists / rebounds + assists / blocks + steals combos; NFL rushing and receiving yards, rush + receiving yards, pass + rush yards, completions, pass attempts and carries. All pass the holdout check except NFL passing yards (chances off by 3.1 points). NBA now also reads 2024-25 for the model.
- NBA forecasts rise when regular teammates are out (`teammates_out`, used for 9 of 12 stats where it beat no adjustment on the holdout). The props board and the MCP read who is out from the ESPN report (Out or Doubtful); the MCP's `TEAMMATES_OUT` note says what the model did with them.
- Store every pregame PrizePicks main line the props scans see (`<sport>/lines/<date>.json`, `lib/sports/line-archive.mjs`, written only when a line is new or moves) and score the model check at those real lines plus the issued props record (`real_lines`); from 300 real lines a stat must beat the last-10 hit rate and a coin flip there too.
- Sports data upgrade (adapter version 2, re-downloads every season once): NFL reads nflverse snap counts (`snaps`, `snap_pct` on every row, plus an all-zero row for a skill player who played snaps but recorded no stat: 408 to 983 games a season were missing), the official NFL injury report (`nfl/injuries.json`, shown beside ESPN's and used only for the week of the player's next game), roster status from `players.csv` (injured reserve and other non-active statuses mark a player out) and keeps the schedule's closing spread and total.
- Game spread and total as a model input (`game_lines`): NFL from the schedule, NBA from the sportsbooks' median spread and total that the props scan now archives every half hour (PropLine `spreads,totals`, in the existing request). With the NFL snap-share trend (`snap_trend`), each is used per stat only where it beat leaving it out on later games by more than noise; on today's data the spread/total helps 8 of 9 NFL stats and snap share 4, and every NFL stat scores better. NBA waits for about 200 archived games. Weather was tested and gave no gain, so it is not used.
- The MCP's `sports_get_player` and `sports_evaluate_entry` return the game's spread and total (`next_game_lines`, `game_lines`, and whether the forecast used them), the official NFL report and roster status (`OFFICIAL_REPORT`, `ROSTER`), `TEAM_CHANGED` when a player moved teams after his last stored game, and a `data_check` block (last update, latest game, cross-check results, warnings) with `STALE_DATA` after a day and a half without a data run. The NFL page and game panels show the spread and total and an NFL Snaps column.
- Data checks in every Sports run (`integrity` in the status record, counts only in the public log): NBA box scores summed against final scores, and up to 15 recent games checked against NBA.com's public box-score files (scores, points / rebounds / assists, missing players).
- A second daily Sports data run at 22:30 UTC, for provider fixes made during the day, the NFL's Friday injury report and roster moves.

### Changed

- Remove sportsbook margin with Shin's method instead of a plain divide-by-total (`shinProbabilities`): it takes more off the longer price, so −200/+160 reads 64.1% instead of 63.4%.
- Bump the Sports model to `sports-model-2`: the daily refit tests a cross-fitted Platt recalibration per stat and applies it only when it beats the raw chances on both Brier and log loss; none passes on today's data, so chances stay raw and the Model check card says why.
- The Model check card folds each stat into one row (verdict and score up front, detail and calibration chart inside) and folds the same-game links behind a count, since NBA now measures 200+ links. The player page shows one line per reason when no stat has a forecast.
- Bump the props ranking to `props-rank-4`: NFL props on the newly modeled stats now rank with a model forecast.
- Sports player charts fill the width of their card (measured on screen, redrawn on resize) instead of stopping at 720 pixels, and the player panel spans the whole page under the games and side column. Opponent names under the bars move to two staggered rows, or every other one, when they would overlap.
- The Sports player page and Pick Builder read the free ESPN injury report like the MCP: the player page shows the player's listing for the next game and the teammates listed out (NBA forecasts count their usual minutes), and a Pick Builder pick says when its player is Out, Doubtful or Questionable. Saving reads the same report as checking, so the model's chance keeps its label. The "Add a pick" line box hints a line near the player's average for the chosen stat instead of always 24.5.
- An NFL prop-record pick whose player has no row in a finished game becomes "No stat line" three days after the game (nflverse leaves out players with no stat), left out of the win rate, instead of waiting for stats forever.
- Props line matching (`playerFor`): short first names match on the same team and last name (Herb / Herbert Jones), a player whose team changed since his last stored game is skipped (`team_changed`) instead of ranked on his old role, an NFL player off the active roster is skipped, and the props job logs match counts and unmatched names (counts only).
- The Sports model's recalibration and input checks compare unrounded scores, so a stat that tied at four decimals no longer flips from run to run. NFL passing yards is now validated; pass + rush yards moves to experimental (calibration error 0.0301 against the 0.03 bar).

### Fixed

- A player listed Out no longer raises their own NBA forecast through the teammates-out lift (MCP `sports_get_player` and Pick Builder), and the props board no longer adds its teammate-DNP rank bump on top of a forecast that already applied the lift.
- Route tests for the Sports player page and Pick Builder use a fake injury feed, so no test reaches ESPN.
- NBA: 33 rows with a missing athlete id are filled from the same game's roster by name, a player listed twice in one game keeps his played row, and seven 2025-26 Chicago games whose box score is one player short of the final are flagged in the status record.

### Docs

- Update docs/sports/README.md (Pick Builder, Stat models, MCP tools, Cost), docs/sports/nba-market-strategy.md, docs/mcp.md and CLAUDE.md for the above.
- Document the Sports data upgrade (sources, checks, model inputs, MCP fields, costs) in `docs/sports/README.md` and the CLAUDE.md Sports paragraph.

### Added

- Add an Owner-only **Sports** section with NBA, NFL and Pick Builder pages in its own sidebar group. This first step is the frame: Today and Tomorrow in Pacific time (with a time-zone picker), each league’s data status, and the Pick Builder steps to come. It reads private JSON under `_stonks/sports/v1/` through the existing Owner session (`/api/auth/me?sports=status`), adds no Vercel function, makes no AI or sportsbook calls, and stays out of the Owner Market Brief, Research Lab evidence and the MCP feature list. `SPORTS_ENABLED=0` turns it off. See `docs/sports/README.md`.
- Connect NFL data to the Owner Sports section. A daily job (`sports-data.yml`) reads free nflverse game and player files for the current and previous season into private storage, without the sportsbook lines the schedule file carries. The NFL page shows today's and tomorrow's games, each team's recent players, player search and game logs with recent averages. A finished season is downloaded once; a bad download never replaces stored data. No AI or sportsbook calls.
- Connect NBA data to the Owner Sports section from hoopR's free ESPN box scores (current and previous season), as a second job in the daily Sports data run. The NBA page shows today's and tomorrow's games, each team's recent players (with anyone who sat out last game flagged), player search and game logs with minutes and the reason for missed games. Both leagues now share one ingest, and stored game logs are packed (a full NBA season shrinks from 16 MB to 4 MB).
- Build the Owner Pick Builder: add 1 to 6 picks from a player on the NBA or NFL page or from its own search, type the payout your app shows (power or flex) and your own chance for each pick, and see the breakeven, the chance all hit if the picks are independent (with the range under any link between them), and the expected profit. Picks from the same game are flagged and not priced. Save it as a paper pick before the games; the journal grades it from the final stats, asks for the app's payout after a push or a player who sat out, and totals paper profit next to what was expected at save. Each pick's history hit rate is shown, and can be copied only from 5 or more games. A shared link can fill in an entry for the owner to check and save. No AI, sportsbook or new Vercel function.
- Add stat forecasts to the Owner Sports section for NBA points and rebounds and NFL passing yards and receptions. Each forecast is expected minutes, pass attempts or targets × the player's rate × the opponent × home or away, with a spread fitted on past misses, so a typed line gets its exact over, under and push chances, an 80% range and what happens if the minutes or targets run 10% lower or higher. The daily data job refits it and checks it on later games it never saw against two simple baselines; a stat is marked as passing only with 300+ checked games, a better score than both and chances off by 3 points or less (on real seasons NBA points and rebounds and NFL receptions pass; passing yards just misses). Player pages show the next game's forecast, Pick Builder picks show the model's chance with a button to use it, saved picks keep it, and the journal compares model and used chances with what happened. NFL keeps three older finished seasons for the model only. No AI calls or new Vercel function.
- Price same-game picks in the Owner Sports Pick Builder. The daily model run measures how picks from one game moved together in past games (the same player's two stats, teammates, opponents) and checks each link on later games against treating the picks as unrelated. Same-game entries now get a chance of all hitting, exact-k chances for flex, a range from how sure each link is, the unrelated number for comparison and the expected value, all from the chances entered and a seeded simulation. On real seasons one NBA player's points and rebounds (+0.28) and an NFL quarterback's passing yards with a teammate's catches (+0.24) pass the check. A pair with no measured link still gets no combined number. No AI calls or new Vercel function.
- Add charts and stats views to the Owner Sports pages. A player's panel now has six charts for any stored stat over the last 5, 10 or 20 games, the season or every stored game: bars around the average (green above, red below, or against a typed line), a trend with a five-game average, hit rates at a ladder of lines, the spread of results, the stat against minutes or volume, and splits by home or away, role, rest, opponent and season, with a facts strip under each. New Player stats and Team stats views sort and chart every player's averages (season, last 10 or last 5; by team, position and games played) and each team's record, scoring and what it allows overall and to each position. The game panel shows each player's last ten games as small bars for any stat, and each Pick Builder pick shows its last ten games against its line. Box scores only; no AI calls or new Vercel function.

- Cover every stored stat and the common combo lines in the Owner Sports section: NBA assists, threes, steals, blocks, turnovers, shooting and rebound splits, PRA, points + rebounds, points + assists, rebounds + assists, blocks + steals and fantasy score; NFL rushing and receiving yards, touchdowns, completions, attempts, interceptions, carries, targets, rush + receiving yards and pass + rush yards.
