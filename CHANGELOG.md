## 2026-07-15

### Changed
- **Top Picks option exits flipped to flat +20% take-profit / −50% stop (scale-out now opt-in).** With the book capped at `PICKS_MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS` (20), the bank-half-and-trail runners held slots open for days and froze enrollment — the record filled to 20 open / 0 closed within three sessions of the 07-10 reset, locking strong new names (AVGO, JPM, AXP) out of the track record. Owner call: resolve fast so positions turn over and slots free up — `PICKS_OPT_STOP_PCT` 0.30 → 0.50, `PICKS_OPT_SCALE_OUT` default flipped to off (set `=1` to restore the trail; already-armed runners keep their trail semantics until they resolve). Client mirrors updated (`accBracketPnlPerContract` gates, the held-position checker's `POS_STOP`). `scripts/build.mjs`, `scripts/render/app-js.mjs`, `docs/top-picks.md`.

### Added
- **MarketBeat as the earnings-call transcript fallback source.** Fool's big-cap coverage has gaps (it never published TSLA's Q1 2026 call at all), so when the Fool quote-page probe yields nothing newer for a name that just reported, discovery now probes MarketBeat's per-ticker earnings hub (`/stocks/<EX>/<SYM>/earnings/` → date-keyed report pages, Quartr-provided full transcripts, free + server-rendered). New parsers `parseMarketBeatReportLinks` / `parseMarketBeatTranscript` (speaker-attributed "Name (Role): speech" text, quarter from the page header, call date from the byline — report URLs are keyed by the CALL date so republication can't spoof recency); same `summarizeEarningsCall` brief, same cache + caps. Index entries + details now carry a per-name `source`, and the detail footer attributes whichever library the brief came from (the renderer already read `d.source` dynamically). `scripts/build.mjs`.

### Fixed
- **Earnings-call discovery picked republished OLD transcripts over the newest call — rank by fiscal quarter, not publication date.** Fool republishes years-old transcripts under fresh URL dates (TSLA's Q3 2024 call resurfaced at a 2026-04-22 URL and buried the real Q4 2025 one, so the tab briefed a two-year-old call). Discovery now orders candidates by the fiscal quarter in the URL slug (publication date only breaks ties / is the fallback), never replaces an index entry with an older-or-equal quarter, and re-checks the article TITLE's quarter after fetch (the slug can lie on republished pages) before spending the Gemini call. Poisoned entries self-heal via the existing newer-print probe + the ect2 re-summary phase-in. `scripts/build.mjs`.

### Added

New PREMIUM keys: `data/earnings-calls.json` (the covered-name index, read-before-wipe) + `data/transcript-<SYM>.json` per-ticker details (upsert-only in the store; gated via the new `PREMIUM_PREFIXES` prefix support in `lib/premium-keys.mjs` — the first directory-gated key family). Each reporting season (the calendar quarter the announcements land in) shows: how many names beat / matched / missed the EPS estimate; how many raised / reaffirmed / cut guidance (a new per-print `guidance` stamp, read from the news-judgment AI's guidance signal in the two weeks after each print — fills in going forward, older quarters show "no read"); how many moved more vs less than the options market's straddle-implied expected move; post-earnings up/down breadth with avg move + 1-week drift; sell-the-news / relief counts (beat-but-fell, missed-but-rose); the biggest gap-ups and gap-downs by name; a full sortable-by-date season table; and a once-per-ET-day **AI season read** (`AI_EARNINGS_MODEL`, default `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` — headline, summary, notable standouts, positive/mixed/negative tone, with a deterministic breadth+beat-rate fallback on keyless builds). ### Perf
- **Bake wall-clock blew up to 60–102 min under a Gemini 503 storm — added an overload cooldown + per-request timeout.** From 2026-07-13 Google's `gemini-3.5-flash`/`gemini-flash-latest` began persistently load-shedding (503 "high demand": 200–300 per run), so ~95% of chart/thesis calls only succeeded after re-probing both sick models with backoff on EVERY call, and some requests hung 5–10 min holding a concurrency slot — the 5-min bake became a 1–2 h bake, queueing the hourly workflows behind it (the shared concurrency group then dropped intervening runs). Two fixes in `scripts/build.mjs`: (1) an **overload cooldown** — `AI_MODEL_COOLDOWN_STRIKES` (3) consecutive 5xx/network failures bench a model for `AI_MODEL_COOLDOWN_MS` (10 min); `aiModelChain` skips benched models from attempt 0 so later calls go straight to the sibling that is actually serving (a success un-benches; expiry re-probes; the all-filtered escape still applies; benched models surface in the AI-health report as `overloadedModels`); (2) a **per-request timeout** — `httpOptions.timeout` = `AI_HTTP_TIMEOUT_MS` (120 s) on every `GoogleGenAI` client (also `scan-unusual.mjs` / `refresh-heatmap.mjs`) so a hung request aborts into the normal retry ladder instead of stalling the pass.
- **Chart-pattern pass concurrency 6 → 12 on the funded project** (`AI_CHART_CONCURRENCY` in `daily.yml`): the twice-daily cache-rollover builds re-read ~139 vision+thinking charts at ~30–60 s each; 12-wide halves that block without changing call volume (~20 RPM vs the 600 pacer).

(2) Articles whose date line omits the weekday ("Date&#10;Oct. 23, 2024, 5:30 p.m. ET") fell through to the URL publication date, which is years off on republished content — Fool re-published Tesla's Q3 2024 call under a 2026-04-22 URL, stamping the brief "held Apr 22, 2026"; a Date-label-anchored fallback pattern now catches these. ### Changed
- **Top Picks exits rebalanced — scale-out + trail replaces the hard +20% win cap, the stock stop is enforced, and the credit-spread stop tightens to −50%.** The track record showed avg loss running ~4× avg win: the flat +20% take-profit hard-capped every winner at the first mark ≥ +20% while overnight/earnings gaps blew losses far past the −30% stop (gates only execute at build-time marks), a payoff needing a >60% win rate against a ~33% record. Now (1) at +20% the position **banks half at the marked price and trails the runner** — breakeven floor, ratcheting to peak−25pts (`trail-stop` status; closed records blend the two halves; `PICKS_OPT_SCALE_OUT=0` restores the flat TP, `PICKS_OPT_TRAIL_GIVEBACK_PCT` tunes the ratchet) — so an armed winner can't round-trip to a net loss but the right tail is no longer amputated; (2) the exit ladder's ~2.5×ATR/structural **stock stop is now enforced in the record** (`hit-stop-under`; it was display-only — `resolvePickOutcome` never read the stored `cut` level, so a name whose stock thesis had broken stayed open until the modeled premium printed −30% at a mark; `PICKS_UNDERLYING_STOP=0` reverts); (3) the credit-vertical stop default drops **−100% → −50% of the credit** (buy-back ≈1.5×), symmetric with the +50% take-profit (the old 2:1 inverted payoff let one stopped spread erase two winners).
