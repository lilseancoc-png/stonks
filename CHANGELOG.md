## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Market briefs now surface the full rates picture — the 2Y/30Y curve and global sovereign bonds — in both the morning and closing read.** The brief macro strip carried only the 10Y; it now shows the whole Treasury curve (**2Y** Fed-path proxy, **10Y**, **30Y**) plus the **2s10s spread** (level + 1-day bp change — the steepening/flattening/inversion signal), so a significant curve move is flagged in both briefs, not just at the 10Y. A new **Global bonds** section (both briefs) pulls foreign sovereign yields from the overnight cross-market sweep — today that's **Japan's 10Y JGB** (the yen-carry / global-duration anchor; Bunds/Gilts when added) — with its 1-day bp move and a `●` "significant" marker at ≥`BRIEF_RATE_SIG_BP` (5bp). Foreign yields are now their **own** section instead of competing in the top-6 overnight strip (which is back to pure equity/FX/commodity/futures, ranked by %), so a low-base JGB move can't be crowded out and isn't double-listed. The morning + closing system prompts gained a RATES & CURVE directive telling the model to call out significant 2Y/curve/foreign-bond moves explicitly. `scripts/build.mjs` (`gatherBriefSignals`/`briefUserMessage`/`briefRenderFields`/`briefSystemPrompt`), `scripts/render/app-js.mjs` (`renderBriefCard` Global-bonds block).
- **Gamma Exposure tab gets a fast-read summary system.** A new verdict header sits above the GEX heatmap and turns the raw dealer-gamma matrix into a one-second read: four badges — **Lean** (where dealer hedging pulls price: ▲ upward / ▼ downward pull toward the pin in long gamma, ⚡ volatile + downside-risk / upside-chase in short gamma), **Regime** (Stable · dealers dampen vs Unstable · dealers amplify), **Volatility** (Subdued → High, with the expected ±%/day move from near-term ATM IV, tilted up in short-gamma regimes and down when firmly pinned), and **Positioning** (call- vs put-γ skew, e.g. "Put-heavy · 27% call γ"). Two new metric tiles — **Magnet (pin)** (the densest-gamma strike price is drawn toward, + its distance from spot) and **Exp. move** (±%/day ≈ $/day from ATM IV) — answer "where is it heading" and "how strong could the moves be." The plain-English takeaway paragraph now weaves the pin target, expected-move magnitude, the flip as the regime trigger, and the call/put walls into one tight read. All client-side + pure (no new fetches); recomputes at the live spot intraday like the rest of the tab. `scripts/render/app-js.mjs` (`gexVerdict`/`gexVerdictHeader`/`gexNearAtmIv`/`gexNarrative`/`gexSummaryHtml`/`computeGex`), `scripts/render/styles-css.mjs` (`.gex-verdict`/`.gex-badge`).
- **Index Calendar now tracks VXUS and VIX alongside SPY/QQQ/IWM.** Two new toggles in the month-grid: **VXUS** (total international ex-US) and **VIX** (volatility). VXUS/^VIX aren't chain tickers, so the bake fetches their daily bars directly (same helper as the overnight sweep) and folds ~1yr of history into `data/index-calendar.json`. **VIX colours are inverted** — a VIX spike is a risk-off day, so green reads "calmer" (VIX down) and red "stress" (VIX up); the displayed % stays the true move, and the per-month green/red tally + compounded-return colour flip to match. `scripts/build.mjs` (`INDEX_CALENDAR_SYMBOLS`/`appendIndexCalendar`), `scripts/render/app-js.mjs`, `scripts/render/styles-css.mjs` (toggle wraps on narrow screens).

### Fixed
- **AI CapEx showed AMZN at "FY2017" and NVDA at "FY2012" — both now read FY2025.** Three bugs compounded: (1) the SEC concept loop tried `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment` first and `break`ed on the **first** concept with any annual data — but AMZN/NVDA re-tagged capex to `PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets` years ago, leaving only **stale legacy** data under the old concept (AMZN stops at FY2017 $7.4B, NVDA at FY2012 $0.14B), so the loop never reached their current data. It now reduces **both** concepts and keeps whichever has the **most recent** fiscal year. (2) AMZN reports ~365-day **trailing-twelve-month** windows in its 10-Qs (`fp=Q1/Q2/Q3`), which the raw 340–380-day filter mistook for a newer fiscal year; the annual set now prefers `fp="FY"` (the 10-K full-year column), and a fresher TTM window is used as the **run-rate** instead of a fake FY. (3) NVDA's fiscal year ends in late January, so the raw end-year labeled it "FY2026" while every Dec/Jun/Sep-ending peer read "FY2025" — the label now shifts a January-ending FY back to the calendar year it predominantly covers. The Mag-7 FY2025 total jumps from a wrong $254B to **$385B**, and the headline now compares the **FY2025 figure to this-year-projected** (TTM run-rate, ~$461B, +19.8%) rather than only the prior-year YoY. Bake-time + render; corrects on the next bake. `scripts/build.mjs` (`reduceCapexFacts`/`buildAiCapexPayload`/`capexFyLabel`), `scripts/render/app-js.mjs` (`renderAiCapex`).
- **Calendar macro-release dates were off by a day for several events.** Two schedule bugs put BLS prints a day late: (1) the jobs report (**Non-Farm Payrolls + Unemployment Rate**) used a plain first-Friday rule, so July 2026 landed on Fri Jul 3 — but BLS pulled it to **Thu Jul 2** because Jul 3 is the observed Independence Day holiday. `empSit` is now **holiday-aware** (steps to the prior business day off a holiday Friday, guarded so a New-Year's-Day Friday doesn't spill into the prior month). (2) The hardcoded **CPI/PPI 2026 baselines were stale by ~a day in most months** (e.g. CPI Jul 15→**14**, Sep 10→11, Oct 15→14, Nov 13→10; PPI Jul 16→**15**, Sep 11→10, Oct 16→15, Dec 11→15) — replaced with the authoritative BLS 2026 dates (verified against the BLS news-release archive + the official PPI release-schedule table). Bake-time only — corrects on the next bake. `scripts/build.mjs` (`computeReleaseSchedule`/`CPI_PPI_SCHEDULE_BASELINE`).

- **Earnings history was a year stale — recent quarters now backfill from Nasdaq.** The Grade tab's Earnings-history card stopped at mid-2025 (e.g. NVDA's last row was a bogus `2025-06-26`, AAPL's was `2025-05-01`) and skipped every quarter since. ### Changed
- **Top Picks — trailing premium take-profit (let winners run; `PICKS_OPT_TRAIL`, default ON).** `resolvePickOutcome` no longer caps every win at a flat +60%. Once the **peak** modeled option gain arms at `PICKS_OPT_TP_PCT` (+60%), the exit floor ratchets to `max(peak·(1−PICKS_OPT_TRAIL_GIVEBACK 0.33), +60%)` — it locks **at least** the old flat TP and trails a runner upward, exiting only on a 33%-of-peak give-back. Long-premium P&L is right-tail-driven, so capping at +60% truncated exactly the trades that pay for the book; this is strictly Pareto over the flat TP (never locks less). Recorded outcome is by the **sign** of the modeled P&L at the trigger, so a violent gap back through the floor between build samples is booked as an honest give-back, not a phantom win. Peak tracked per-pick as `optMfePct`. `PICKS_OPT_TRAIL=0` reverts to the flat TP. Needs the entry-option snapshot (gate-era picks).
- **Top Picks — tighter spread gate + harder spread weighting (`PICKS_CLEAN_MAX_SPREAD_PCT` 0.12→0.10).** Bid/ask is a first-order tax on a single-leg long (the loss diagnostic measured ~25% round-trip on the legacy OTM picks). The roster spread cap is tightened to 10% (now env-overridable) and the contract composite weights spread far harder (0.13→0.24), saturating its penalty at `PICKS_SPREAD_PEN_REF` (10%) so the tightest-spread survivor wins. Verified the current roster is unaffected (LLY/SLV/EWY/NKE contracts all sit at 3.9–8.7%).
- **Top Picks — IV rank is now a GATE, not just a nudge (`PICKS_IVRANK_VETO`, default ON).** `computeEntryTiming` upgrades the rich-premium read from a soft con to a **strong con** once a name's own-history IV percentile clears `PICKS_IVRANK_VETO` (90) — which blocks a `go` (demotes to `wait`, not enrolled) and shaves the timing contribution. The engine no longer endorses a **naked long bought at the 90th+ percentile of a name's own vol**, including a risk-off "tactical put" bought into a VIX/IV spike (long vol after the move — the exact failure the review flagged). Side-aware (weakens conviction for whichever side, never flips it); set `PICKS_IVRANK_VETO=0` to disable. Bounded behavior change — it can only *reduce* endorsement/conviction at vol extremes, never mint a pick.
- **Top Picks — debit verticals auto-engage in rich-IV / negative-edge conditions (`PICKS_VERT_AUTO`, DARK).** New auto policy: when on, `pickContractForPick` finances a rich-IV long (IV rank ≥ `PICKS_VERT_IVRANK` 70, or ≥ `PICKS_VERT_NEGEDGE_IVRANK` 50 on a measured-negative-edge book) as a **debit vertical** instead of buying naked premium — without flipping the master `PICKS_VERTICALS`. Sizing + the modeled-exit repricer are now fully vertical-aware (premium = `netDebit`, **net delta** across both legs, `modelVerticalExit`). **Dark by default** (`PICKS_VERT_AUTO=1` enables): shipping it live needs the pick card to render two legs (it shows the net debit + breakeven today, not the spread structure / capped max-profit), and the loss attribution still shows losses are direction-driven. Negative-edge measured via the shared `realizedOptionEdge` helper (refactored out of `computeEdgeScale`).

- **Top Picks — collinearity-collapse decorrelation (dark by default).** `computeCrossSectionalScores` can now collapse correlated converted-signal clusters (`SIGNAL_CLUSTER`: growth / value / momentum / mean-rev / flow) by scaling each signal's contribution `1/√K` (K = its cluster's firing signals for that name), so a cluster of correlated signals contributes like ~one factor instead of K× — capping the loading on whatever common beta (usually the Tech/AI complex) the universe shares (the audit's root-cause #1: z-scoring fixes each marginal but never touches the covariance). `PICKS_DECORRELATE` default **OFF**: it changes the grade scale (roster 4→1 on the committed sample), and an unvalidated score change won't ship live on a losing engine — the scaling is itself a forward-validation target.
- **Top Picks P0.3 — premium-space exits.** `resolvePickOutcome` now resolves on the MODELED option P&L *before* the underlying-level TP/cut: take profit at `PICKS_OPT_TP_PCT` (+60%) and cut at `PICKS_OPT_STOP_PCT` (−40%) of entry premium. A symmetric underlying stop maps to a wildly asymmetric option move, so the premium stop truncates the −60% tail the structural cut lets run. Levels are flat (vol/DTE-blind) for now — the AXIS (premium, not stock) is the first-principles fix; the levels get regime-aware once a forward sample exists. The structural underlying cut stays as the broken-level exit (with a note that it can't be honored intraday on a gap — the premium stop is the real bound).
- **Top Picks P1.3 — edge governor.** `applyPickSizing` scales the deployed gross by the trailing realized OPTION expectancy (`computeEdgeScale` over the prior `closed` set, threaded from `picksAccuracyPrev`): full gross at a ≥0% edge, ramping to a floor (`PICKS_EDGE_SCALE_MIN`) at a deeply negative one (`PICKS_EDGE_FULL_CUT_EXP`), a conservative default below `PICKS_EDGE_MIN_N` results. A measured-negative-edge book no longer redeploys 80% gross every build. `rosterMeta.edgeScale` records the multiplier.
- **Top Picks P1.4 — liquidity as a hard candidacy gate.** `buildTopPicks` computes the tradeable (`requireClean`) contract up front and DROPS names with none BEFORE ranking, so the roster ranks the universe of *tradeable* names (not merely graded ones) and the candidate slice can't be filled by high-grade names we can't trade out of. `rosterMeta.untradeable` counts the drops; the contract is reused in the loop (no double compute).
- **Top Picks P1.5 — premium-at-risk sizing.** The option-risk denominator in `applyPickSizing` now adds the theta bled over a typical hold (`PICKS_SIZE_HOLD_DAYS`) + a modeled IV mean-reversion drop (`vega × min(IV−HV, PICKS_SIZE_IV_DROP_CAP)`) on top of the delta-to-stop loss, so short-DTE / rich-IV contracts size smaller than a calm one with the same delta. `vega` is now returned on the contract.
- **Top Picks P2 — term-structure gate + earnings-eve exit.** `computeEntryTiming` adds a soft con when the IV term structure is in backwardation (front-month richer than the back by ≥ `PICKS_TIMING_BACKWARDATION`) — buying the most expensive point on the curve. `resolvePickOutcome` adds a forward earnings-eve exit: if a print creeps within `PICKS_EARNINGS_EXIT_DAYS` of the mark, close at the pre-crush modeled mark rather than ride the IV crush.
- **Top Picks P0.1 — honest fills in the modeled option P&L (enter at ask, exit at bid).** `modelOptionExit` now enters at the contract's ASK and haircuts the BS exit toward the BID using the stamped `spreadPct`, instead of a free mid-to-mid round trip — charging the 12–18% bid/ask the contract selector admits. `pickContractForPick` now returns `spreadPct`, and the accuracy enroll snapshot stamps `bid`/`ask`/`spreadPct` so the repricer (and the live theta-stop's unrealized-loss read) can use them. Legacy entries without the quote fall back to mid (graceful). This stops `optionExpectancyPct` from flattering every result the moment it populates.

### Changed
- **Chart-pattern detector now rates the 1-MONTH INTRADAY chart (was daily) + scales reliably.** The pattern that feeds the Top Picks grade is now read off ~1 month of 30-minute intraday bars instead of daily closes — short-horizon reversals (an intraday head-and-shoulders, etc.) live there and vanish into a single spike on daily bars, so the detector was structurally blind to them. It re-rates **~2×/day** (open + midday, an ET AM/PM cache bucket) since intraday bars change every 30 min, and on the next bake re-reads every name once (`img4` cache version). Also fixes a real reliability bug: the multimodal pass fired ~137 chart images in one burst, rate-limiting Gemini so **~half the universe (66/137, including MSFT) ended a build with no pattern verdict at all** — it now runs at a bounded concurrency (`AI_CHART_CONCURRENCY`, default 6) and **falls back to a text-only read** when an image call fails, so a burst can't leave a ticker undetected. The detector image is the clean regular-session intraday line with ≈1-day/≈3-day smoothing overlays.
- **Chart-pattern detector now predicts a head-and-shoulders (and the other 6) *while it's forming*, not just after it completes.** The detector reports a `stage` — **forming** (the shape is partially built, e.g. left shoulder + a higher head and price rolling into a right shoulder, but the neckline hasn't broken) or **confirmed** (the decisive break has happened) — plus the `neckline` level and one-line `confirm` / `invalidate` / `target` reads. The Technicals card surfaces a "⚠ Forming" badge with those levels so you can act before confirmation. Scoring stays conservative: a **confirmed** pattern scores flat ±1 by direction as before, a **forming** one scores **0** (early warning, surfaced but doesn't move a pick until price actually breaks). The detector image is also zoomed to a ~75-session window (was ~140) with 20/50-day SMAs so a building reversal is large and legible, and the per-ticker cache key is bumped (`img3`) so every prior verdict re-reads once.
- **Chart-pattern detector now *sees* the chart instead of reading numbers.** `generateChartPattern` rendered the daily series as a plain text table and asked the model to infer geometry from a column of numbers — a visual task done blind, which is why clear formations (e.g. NOW's head-and-shoulders) came back "None". It now renders the same window to a PNG (new dependency-free `lib/chart-image.mjs`, pure JS + Node `zlib`) and sends it as a multimodal image alongside the numeric series (kept for exact level/date citations), upgrades the model from Flash-Lite to **Flash** (`AI_CHART_MODEL`, since Lite reads images poorly) with a small thinking budget, and version-bumps the per-ticker cache key (`img2`) so every cached pre-image verdict is re-read once. Only the moving-average overlays the window can actually compute are named to the model (a 140-bar series has a 50-day line but no 200-day one). Image render is best-effort — a failure falls back to the old text-only path, so the bake never breaks on it. Cost stays low: the chart-pattern cache means this fires ~once/ticker/trading day.
- **Top Picks now only ever recommends a contract the grader itself would pass.** Previously `pickContractForPick` shipped the best contract it found even when its own grade fns called it `bad` (2 of 10 live picks — ASML, CLS — were `overall:"bad"`), and its hard gates were *looser* than the Grade tab's "bad" lines (spread admitted to 18% but the grader flags >15%; OI floor 50 but liquidity is "thin/bad" <100; no theta gate at all), so clicking a pick's "Grade this contract →" frequently tripped a hard-fail and surfaced the "Try this instead / Switch to this contract" swap. The roster path now runs with `requireClean` (`buildTopPicks` only): buffered self-consistency gates — spread ≤12%, OI ≥100, DTE ≥21, theta ≤2.5%/day — that sit *inside* every grade-bad line, plus an explicit refusal of any candidate the grade fns would call bad (and a return-null invariant). A name with no clean contract is dropped and the next conviction-ranked candidate backfills. The delta band stays 0.20–0.40 (an OTM pick that can still appreciate; `|Δ|` 0.20–0.40 is never a grader hard-fail) and is no longer folded into the contract's `overall` rollup (it always graded "fair" and was capping every pick at "fair"). The universe-wide `autoPick`/"★ Top-Picks grade" banner keeps the looser documented gates, so the on-screen spec text is unchanged (#317).
- **A vetted Top Pick is never offered a swap.** When the user clicks through from a pick card, the Grade tab now trusts the bake's `requireClean` verdict instead of re-deriving it from live quotes: it suppresses the mechanical hard-fails / "Try this instead" alternative for that exact contract, shows a "Top Pick · contract vetted" verdict, and frames any live deterioration as transient quote drift (re-checked each build) rather than condemning the pick. Disengages the moment the user changes strike/expiry/type, and never applies to the `autoPick` banner (whose contracts can legitimately be bad) (#317).
