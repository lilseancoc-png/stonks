04How membership works

Premium is unlocked through our Discord server. There's no separate stonks account — your Discord membership is your access.

  1. Join the Discord and get the member role. Premium access rides on a role in our server. Members get it automatically; ask in the server if you don't see it yet.
  2. Log in with Discord. Hit Log in (or the "Log in" button in the site header). We check your server membership and role — nothing else.
  3. The premium tabs unlock. Once you hold the role, the eight members-only tabs and their data open up. A non-member just sees a polite upsell where those tabs would be — the rest of the site never locks.
Privacy & sessions: we only read your Discord user ID, username, and the roles relevant to access. Login mints a short-lived, secure session cookie — sign out anytime. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

05Good to know

  • Refreshed through the day. The data bakes multiple times each trading day — picks, grades, narratives, heatmap, and flow all update on a schedule, not just once.
  • Sources. Quotes and chains come from Yahoo Finance; macro from the Fed, BLS, Treasury, and SEC; sentiment from CNN's Fear & Greed index. AI theses and news takes are generated by Google Gemini.
  • Greeks are computed locally. Black-Scholes math runs in your browser against the live risk-free rate — nothing about a contract you grade is uploaded.
  • Not financial advice. stonks is a research and educational tool. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Options carry risk; do your own diligence.
